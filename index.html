The Sacred Hierarchy

Set each title and the lifetime point total required to reach it.

Rank progression · lowest to highest
The first rank must require 0 points. Every rank above it must have a higher threshold than the previous rank. A member's title updates automatically when their total crosses a threshold, and can fall if points are deducted.

How progression works

Points are cumulative across all dates. For example, if a member has 72 points and the next title begins at 75, the progress bar shows 72 / 75. Deductions reduce the lifetime total. The daily ledger lets you see exactly when points were added or removed.
